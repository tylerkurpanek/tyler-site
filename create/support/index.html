Common questions

How do I turn off the lock screen timer? Open the More tab and switch off "Show timer on lock screen."

How do I restore my data on a new phone? Sign in on the More tab and enter your recovery key when asked. Without the recovery key, backups can't be unlocked.

How do I delete my account? Open the More tab and tap "Delete account." This deletes your account and cloud data; data on your phone stays.
